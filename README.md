Smart Power Meter with Energy Monitoring & Load Control

2nd Place – National Award, Sri Lanka Inventors Commission

Project Description
I designed and developed a Smart Power Meter for real-time energy monitoring, billing, and safety, specially targeted for rental houses, boarding houses, rental shops, and home energy management. The device measures and displays key electrical parameters including voltage, current, frequency, power, power factor, and energy consumption (kWh) using an integrated dual-display system. Power input and output are clearly separated to ensure safe and accurate monitoring of connected loads. In addition to on-device monitoring, the system supports computer-based data analysis, where users can view load curve charts, detailed energy usage, and automatic electricity bill calculation in Sri Lankan Rupees, making energy cost tracking simple and transparent.
A key feature of this system is energy usage limit control. Users can set a maximum kWh limit, and once the defined energy usage is reached, the device automatically disconnects the power supply, helping prevent overconsumption and ensuring fair usage in shared or rental environments.
The Smart Power Meter also includes advanced safety features. A built-in gas/fire detection system immediately cuts off the power supply during hazardous conditions, improving electrical safety.
To ensure reliability, the device operates with a battery backup, allowing continuous functionality during power outages without data loss. During outages, it provides 12V and 5V emergency outputs to charge routers and mobile devices, along with an LDR-based LED light for visibility in dark conditions.
This project combines energy monitoring, safety, billing, and backup power into a single, cost-effective solution and was recognized with 2nd Place at the National Awards by the Sri Lanka Inventors Commission.
